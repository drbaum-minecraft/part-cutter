Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026
Scope: Part Cutter, current Beta 1.0 release (build 14).

Your videos are processed on your device. Part Cutter does not upload selected videos, audio tracks or editing text to the developer's servers. The current release does not display ads or offer active Google Play purchases.

2. What the app processes

You choose a main video and, when using the corresponding editing features, additional videos through Android's system media picker. You can also open or share a video into Part Cutter from another app. The app reads the selected files, their audio tracks, file names, sizes, duration, dimensions and orientation to display previews, calculate parts and create exports.

Video and audio content can contain personal information about you or other people. The app also processes the text you enter, editing positions, effects, output settings and names you give to settings profiles or exported videos. These are used for the editing functions you request.

Part Cutter has no own account or login, developer-operated cloud storage, external video-processing API, integrated Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics, or own analytics/tracking service. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location access. Audio analysis reads the selected video's existing audio; it does not record your microphone.

3. Local video processing and exports

Preview, trimming, audio analysis, text and video effects, and export run locally using Android media components and AndroidX Media3. Selected videos are not sent to the developer or to a video-processing server.

The app can retain permission to reopen a selected file. A video opened from another app may be copied into private app storage so the project can continue to use it. Temporary export files are stored privately and normally removed after processing; interrupted export work is cleaned up when the app recovers.

Finished MP4 videos are saved in shared device storage under Movies/Part Cutter/Part Cutter Export …/ and registered with Android MediaStore so compatible gallery apps can display them. The in-app export library reads the app's export folder to show names, duration and size and lets you play, rename and share exports.

If you choose Share, Android gives the receiving app access to the selected exported videos. That app's handling, including any upload it performs, is governed by its own privacy policy. If a media picker or file provider retrieves a video from cloud storage, that provider's download and privacy practices are separate from Part Cutter's local processing. Gallery or backup apps you use may also sync exported videos independently.

5. Local storage, backup and deletion

The app stores editing settings, language and theme preferences, onboarding status, saved profiles (including names, icons, update times, export usage counts and optionally references to additional videos), and the current project's selected file references, text, trim points and positions. Local export recovery information records progress, output references and errors. The beta Premium test switch and completed-export counters are also stored locally. These counts are not sent to a developer analytics service.

Android backup and device transfer are enabled for the settings/profile file, onboarding preference and app-language preference. Depending on your device and Android backup settings, these may be backed up by your system's backup provider, including Google. Saved profiles may contain your chosen names, text prefixes and optional video references. The app's backup rules exclude the active project, imported video copies, temporary export work, export recovery records and the beta Premium state. This does not control separate backups made by your gallery or other apps.

Settings and project data remain until changed, overwritten or removed with Android's Clear storage function or uninstall. You can delete individual saved profiles in the app. Completed exports and original selected videos are in shared storage and are not automatically deleted by uninstalling Part Cutter; delete them through your gallery or file manager. Backed-up preferences may be restored when reinstalling; manage backups separately in your device settings.

6. Google libraries, advertising and Premium

The current release bundles Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform for consent, plus Google Play Billing. Advertising is disabled in the release configuration: the app does not initiate ad loading, display ads or request the advertising consent form. Google Play purchases are also inactive because no Premium product is configured. The sideloaded beta's optional Premium test switch is local and is not a purchase.

The bundled advertising SDK includes startup components and supporting Google libraries; disabled ad requests should not be read as a guarantee that every third-party component has no technical activity. Google's SDK documentation describes processing of IP addresses, device/account identifiers, interactions and diagnostic information for its advertising services. This policy does not claim those services are actively used to show ads in this build. See Google's SDK data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.

No payment-card details are requested or stored by Part Cutter. Any activation of real ads or Google Play purchasing in a later release requires an updated policy reflecting that release's actual configuration.

7. Sharing, support and this website

Part Cutter's own editing code does not transmit your videos, editing text, projects or local usage counters to the developer, and the developer does not sell that content. The exceptions to purely local handling are actions or system services you choose, such as sharing with another app, Android backup, visiting this website or contacting support.

If you email support, your email address, message and any attachments you voluntarily send are processed to answer your request through the Outlook email service. Do not send private videos or other sensitive information unless needed for your request. Support correspondence is retained while needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal obligations. Provider processing and international transfers are described in Microsoft's Privacy Statement.

This static privacy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Viewing it sends the normal web request, including your IP address, to GitHub. GitHub logs IP addresses for security purposes even without a GitHub login. The developer does not add cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, external fonts or third-party scripts to this page. The language switch does not use cookies or local storage. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for hosting, retention and international processing.

8. Your choices and rights

You control which videos to select, whether to grant optional notifications, whether to share exports or contact support, and your device backup settings. The developer cannot remotely access or delete files held only on your device.

Where applicable data-protection law provides them, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data held about you, object to relevant processing and complain to a competent supervisory authority. You may withdraw consent where processing relies on it, without affecting earlier lawful processing. Contact partcutter.help@outlook.com to exercise rights relating to data the developer holds.

Where the GDPR applies, processing necessary to provide the requested app functions or answer a related support request relies on Article 6(1)(b); other support and security processing relies on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f), and mandatory retention on Article 6(1)(c). External providers describe their own legal bases in their policies. No developer-operated automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects are made about users.

9. Children

The app does not ask for age, create children's accounts or offer a feature for sending children's personal information to the developer. Local video processing applies equally to any selected content, which may include children. A parent or guardian can contact support if a child has sent personal information by email and request its deletion, subject to applicable law. This paragraph does not set the app's Google Play target-audience or age rating.

10. Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when the app's actual data practices change. The date above identifies the latest update. For privacy questions or support, contact partcutter.help@outlook.com.

2. Welche Daten die App verarbeitet

Du wählst über die Android-Medienauswahl ein Hauptvideo und bei den entsprechenden Bearbeitungsfunktionen zusätzliche Videos aus. Außerdem kannst du ein Video aus einer anderen App in Part Cutter öffnen oder an Part Cutter teilen. Die App liest die ausgewählten Dateien, ihre Tonspuren, Dateinamen, Dateigrößen, Dauer, Abmessungen und Ausrichtung, um Vorschauen anzuzeigen, Parts zu berechnen und Exporte zu erstellen.

Bild- und Toninhalte können personenbezogene Informationen über dich oder andere Personen enthalten. Die App verarbeitet außerdem deine eingegebenen Texte, Bearbeitungspositionen, Effekte, Ausgabeeinstellungen und Namen für Einstellungsprofile oder exportierte Videos. Diese Daten dienen den von dir angeforderten Bearbeitungsfunktionen.

Part Cutter hat kein eigenes Benutzerkonto oder Login, keinen vom Entwickler betriebenen Cloud-Speicher, keine externe Videoverarbeitungs-API, kein integriertes Firebase Analytics oder Crashlytics und keinen eigenen Analyse- oder Trackingdienst. Die App fordert keinen Zugriff auf Kamera, Mikrofon, Kontakte oder Standort an. Die Audioanalyse liest die vorhandene Tonspur des ausgewählten Videos und nimmt dein Mikrofon nicht auf.

3. Lokale Videoverarbeitung und Exporte

Vorschau, Zuschnitt, Audioanalyse, Text- und Videoeffekte sowie Export erfolgen lokal mit Android-Medienkomponenten und AndroidX Media3. Ausgewählte Videos werden nicht an den Entwickler oder einen Videoverarbeitungsserver gesendet.

Die App kann die Zugriffsfreigabe zum erneuten Öffnen einer ausgewählten Datei behalten. Ein aus einer anderen App geöffnetes Video kann in den privaten App-Speicher kopiert werden, damit das Projekt es weiterhin nutzen kann. Temporäre Exportdateien liegen im privaten Speicher und werden normalerweise nach der Verarbeitung entfernt; unterbrochene Exportarbeit wird bei der Wiederherstellung bereinigt.

Fertige MP4-Videos werden im gemeinsamen Gerätespeicher unter Movies/Part Cutter/Part Cutter Export …/ gespeichert und in Android MediaStore eingetragen, damit passende Galerie-Apps sie anzeigen können. Die Exportbibliothek in der App liest den eigenen Exportordner, zeigt Namen, Dauer und Größe an und ermöglicht Abspielen, Umbenennen und Teilen.

Wenn du „Teilen“ wählst, gewährt Android der empfangenden App Zugriff auf die ausgewählten Exportvideos. Für deren Verarbeitung, einschließlich möglicher Uploads, gilt die Datenschutzerklärung dieser App. Wenn die Medienauswahl oder ein Dateianbieter ein Video aus einem Cloud-Speicher abruft, sind dessen Download und Datenschutzregeln von der lokalen Verarbeitung in Part Cutter zu unterscheiden. Auch von dir verwendete Galerie- oder Sicherungs-Apps können Exportvideos unabhängig synchronisieren.

Gesicherte Einstellungen können bei einer Neuinstallation wiederhergestellt werden; verwalte Sicherungen separat in den Geräteeinstellungen.
